## Q19, word for word (start and the name section)

> "Pinch" most commonly refers to **Pinch: Recipe Library** (`pinch-app.io`), an AI-powered recipe organizer designed to turn social media cooking videos into structured, cookable recipes. *(Note: Because "Pinch" is a popular name for cooking tools, there are also a few other apps with similar names, such as **Pinch** at `getpinch.io` and **Pinch It! Recipe Box**.)*
>
> **Video-to-Recipe AI Extraction:** Share a cooking video from TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube directly to Pinch. Instead of just scraping the caption, its AI watches the video, listens to the spoken audio, and reads on-screen text to transcribe the full ingredient list and step-by-step instructions.
>
> … **Multilingual Support:** Supports English, Spanish, Hebrew, Arabic (including right-to-left formatting), and Russian …
>
> **Pricing Model:** Unlike many recipe apps that require a monthly subscription, Pinch operates on a freemium credit model …
>
> **Pinch (`getpinch.io` / `getpinchapp.com`):** A competing AI recipe saver with similar TikTok/Instagram extraction, natural-language "vibe" search, household sharing, and a ~$3.99/month Pro subscription.
> **Pinch It! Recipe Box:** The companion app for the *Just A Pinch* recipe community.

## Q20, word for word (pricing part)

> **Free Tier:** Every account automatically receives **10 free recipe extraction credits per month** (increased from 3 in version 1.0.8). Adding your own recipes manually, editing saved recipes, or restoring from a backup file does not consume credits.
> **Paid Credit Packs (No Subscription):** 10 Recipe Credits: $1.99 · 30 Recipe Credits: $4.99 · 100 Recipe Credits: $7.99
