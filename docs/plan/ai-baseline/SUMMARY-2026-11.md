## What we learned

1. **The assistants can find Pinch's facts, but nobody recommends Pinch.** Gemini knew every feature and price, which it got from our site and store listings. The neutral answers are driven by third-party "best apps" articles, and Pinch isn't in them.
2. **Competitors write their own "best apps" posts, and the assistants cite them.** CookBook, Pluck and CookinBuddy each publish a "best app to save recipes from TikTok" list with themselves at #1. Perplexity used them as its only sources.
3. **The name collides with other apps.** getpinch.io is a direct competitor (AI recipe saver, TikTok and Instagram, $3.99/month). pinch.kitchen and Pinch It! Recipe Box are also out there. ChatGPT mixed them up: it described the wrong app in Q19 and showed a green logo in Q03. Gemini kept them apart by using "Pinch: Recipe Library".
4. **ReciMe owns social video.** It was #1 or near the top in every TikTok and Instagram answer. Both assistants single out reading spoken and on-screen ingredients as what matters, and Pinch does that.
5. **The diet questions are open.** Small single-purpose converters win them (RecipeFix, VeggieOS, Makmak), and the assistants offer to do the conversion themselves.
6. **Unknown features:** cookbook photo scanning (Q07), the pantry-aware shopping list (Q12), the fridge (Q13), and JSON backup and restore for switching apps (Q17).
7. **ChatGPT's one recommendation came from an article**, "I tried 4 viral recipe apps, and there's a clear winner". Find it, and learn who wrote it.

## What this changes in the plan

**Step 2 (website), in priority order:**

1. Use the name "Pinch: Recipe Library" everywhere (title, JSON-LD `name` and `alternateName`, `sameAs` to both store listings and social accounts, `logo`), and add an FAQ line telling us apart from other apps called Pinch.
2. Write our own honest "best apps to save recipes from TikTok / Instagram / YouTube" guides, as the competitors do, and include the competitors fairly.
3. Add a page for making any recipe vegan, gluten-free or dairy-free.
4. Lead with "reads spoken ingredients and on-screen text" on the video pages.
5. Add pages for switching apps ("Move from Paprika / ReciMe / Mela to Pinch"), cookbook photos, and pantry and fridge.

**Step 4 (outreach) targets:** Vima Fitness (best recipe apps 2026), the "I tried 4 viral recipe apps" article, Beebom, MacStories, iMore, The Sweet Setup, Hedgehog Chef.
