Pinterest boards and Facebook saves fill up fast. A pin leads to a blog with three pop-ups and a life story before the ingredients. A reel shows the recipe once, quickly, and is gone from your feed the next day.

Pinch turns both into a written recipe in your Library: ingredients with amounts, numbered steps, and a shopping list.

## Save a Pinterest pin

1. **Copy the pin's link.** In the Pinterest app, tap **Share**, then **Copy link**. On a computer, copy the address from your browser.
2. **Open Pinch, go to Snap, and paste it.** On your phone you can also tap **Share** on the pin and pick Pinch.
3. **That's it.** The recipe goes straight into your Library as soon as it's ready.

**How Pinch reads a pin:** most recipe pins link to the recipe's own website. Pinch follows that link and reads the recipe from the site, without the pop-ups. If the pin has no link, or the site can't be read, Pinch reads the pin itself: its description, and for video pins, the video.

Short links (pin.it) and Pinterest's country sites work too.

## Save a Facebook reel or post

1. **Copy the link.** On the reel or post, tap **Share**, then **Copy link**.
2. **Open Pinch, go to Snap, and paste it.** Or tap **Share** and pick Pinch.
3. **That's it.** The recipe goes straight into your Library.

Pinch reads the caption, the comments (where creators often put the recipe), and for reels, what's said and shown in the video. A saved reel keeps its video, so you can cook along with it next to the steps.

It works for **public** reels and posts. A post in a private group, or one shared only with friends, can't be read. Screenshot the recipe instead and choose the screenshot in **Snap**.

## Once it's saved

Your recipe works like any other in Pinch:

- **Scale the servings**, or switch between cups and grams.
- **Send the ingredients to your shopping list**, grouped by aisle.
- **Remix it**, for example vegan, gluten-free or healthier. See [how to make any recipe vegan, gluten-free or dairy-free](/guides/make-any-recipe-vegan-gluten-free/).
- **Add it to your week's plan.** See [how to plan a week of meals](/guides/weekly-meal-plan/).
- **Sort it into a collection** with your recipes from TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.

## What it costs

Each recipe you save uses one recipe credit, and every account gets **10 free a month**. If Pinch can't find a recipe, you don't pay. Saving the same link twice opens the recipe you already have, at no cost.

## Common questions

### Can I save a whole Pinterest board at once?

Not yet. Save the pins one at a time, starting with the ones you'll actually cook. It keeps your Library small enough to use.

### What about recipes shared in a WhatsApp group?

Pinch can read a whole exported chat and bring in every recipe link, written recipe and photo at once. See [how to save the recipes from a WhatsApp chat](/guides/save-recipes-from-whatsapp/).

### Does it work for TikTok, Instagram and YouTube?

Yes, the same way. See [how to save a recipe from TikTok](/guides/save-tiktok-recipes/) and [how to turn a YouTube cooking video into a recipe](/guides/youtube-video-to-recipe/).
