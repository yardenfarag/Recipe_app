You've saved dozens of recipes, it's 7 pm, and you're staring into the fridge. Which of those recipes can you actually make tonight?

Pinch answers that from your pantry. Take a few photos of your fridge and cupboards, and Pinch lists what you have. Then **What can I cook?** ranks **the recipes you've already saved** by what's at home, and shows what each one is still missing.

## Step 1: Fill your pantry from photos

1. **Go to Settings → Pantry and tap "Scan my kitchen".**
2. **Pick a place**, like Fridge, Freezer or Cupboards, and **take a photo**. Add as many as you like. Each photo is read while you take the next one.
3. **Tap "Check the list".** Untick anything that isn't there, fix a name with the pencil, and add what Pinch missed. Then save.

It takes about two minutes, and you only do it once. After that, a quick rescan now and then keeps it right.

**On a computer?** Upload photos or drop them on the page. Or choose **Use your phone's camera** and point your phone at the code: the photos you take show up on your computer, with no app or sign-in needed on the phone.

## Step 2: Ask "What can I cook?"

1. **Open your Library and tap "What can I cook?".**
2. **Read the list.** Recipes with the fewest missing ingredients come first. Each one shows how much you have, like "6 of 8 at home", or **You have everything**.
3. **Pick a recipe.** Tap **Add missing to list** to put just the few things you need on your shopping list, or open the recipe and start cooking.

It's instant, and you don't need a new photo each time.

## Keep the pantry up to date

You don't have to rescan every day. Pinch keeps the pantry close to right as you go:

- **When you shop:** check off an item on your list, and Pinch offers to add it to your pantry.
- **When you cook:** tap **I cooked this**, and Pinch asks **Used anything up?** Tick what's gone.
- **When you rescan:** Pinch shows what was in your pantry but not in the new photos, so you can tick what's gone.

## Tips for better photos

- **Open the door all the way** and **turn on the light**.
- **Crowded shelf? One shelf per photo.** Pinch only sees what's in front.
- **Check the list before you save.** Pinch can miss things or get them wrong, especially when labels are hidden.

## Why it uses your saved recipes

Pinch doesn't invent random dishes from your leftovers. It looks through the recipes you already chose to save, the ones you actually want to cook, and puts the best fits first. That means tonight's dinner is something you were already excited about.

Save more recipes for better matches. New to Pinch? Start with a few: [from TikTok](/guides/save-tiktok-recipes/), [from YouTube](/guides/youtube-video-to-recipe/) or [from a cookbook photo](/guides/cookbook-photo-to-recipe/).

## When you're missing something

- **Swap it.** Every ingredient has a **Swap** button that suggests alternatives and rewrites the steps.
- **Add it to your list.** **Add missing to list** puts only what you need on your [shopping list](/guides/shopping-list-pantry/).

## Plan the whole week around it

Your pantry also feeds the meal plan. Pick **Use up my fridge** when you plan the week, and Pinch leans even harder on what you already have. See [how to plan a week of meals](/guides/weekly-meal-plan/).

## What it costs

Scanning your kitchen and **What can I cook?** are free and don't use recipe credits. You can scan up to 25 photos a day.

## Common questions

### Does it suggest new recipes?

No. **What can I cook?** ranks the recipes you've saved, so every suggestion is a dish you already wanted to cook. The meal plan can add new ones from the Cooking Hub if you want them.

### Do I need an account?

Yes. Your pantry is kept on your account, and it looks through your saved recipes.

### Can the people I live with use the same pantry?

Yes. Set up a household and you share the pantry and the shopping list. See [how to share a shopping list with your family](/guides/share-shopping-list-with-family/).
