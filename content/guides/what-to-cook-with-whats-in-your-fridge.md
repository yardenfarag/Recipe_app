You've saved dozens of recipes, it's 7 pm, and you're staring into the fridge. Which of those recipes can you actually make tonight?

Pinch answers that from a photo. Snap your fridge or counter, and Pinch ranks **the recipes you've already saved** by what you have. Each one shows what you'd still need.

## How to do it

1. **Open your Library and tap "Fridge".**
2. **Tap "Take or choose a photo"** and snap your open fridge, or your counter with what's out.
3. **Wait a moment** while Pinch is "Matching your recipes…".
4. **Pick a recipe.** Each match shows a short reason and **Still need**, the few things you're missing. Tap one to open it.

## Why it uses your saved recipes

Pinch doesn't invent random dishes from your leftovers. It looks through the recipes you already chose to save, the ones you actually want to cook, and puts the best fits first. That means tonight's dinner is something you were already excited about.

## Tips

- **Open the doors wide** and use good light, so Pinch can see what's on the shelves.
- **Take one photo of the fridge and one of the counter** if things are spread out, and try both.
- **Save more recipes for better matches.** The more you have in your library, the more likely one fits what's in the fridge. New to Pinch? Start by saving a few: [from TikTok](/guides/save-tiktok-recipes/), [from YouTube](/guides/youtube-video-to-recipe/) or [from a cookbook photo](/guides/cookbook-photo-to-recipe/).

## When you're missing something

- **Swap it.** Every ingredient has a **Swap** button that suggests alternatives and rewrites the steps.
- **Add it to your list.** Add just the missing items to your [shopping list](/guides/shopping-list-pantry/).

## What it costs

Fridge matching is free and doesn't use recipe credits. You can use it up to 8 times a day.

## Common questions

### Does it suggest new recipes?

No. It ranks the recipes you've saved, so every suggestion is a dish you already wanted to cook.

### Do I need an account?

Yes, fridge matching works when you're signed in, because it looks through your saved recipes.

### Does it work on my computer?

Yes. In the browser at [pinch-app.io/app](/app/), choose a photo from your computer.
