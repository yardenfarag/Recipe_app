Mom's lentil soup is somewhere in the family group, between birthday wishes and 400 photos of the dog. A friend sent a great TikTok last spring. Your aunt typed out her cake recipe in three messages. Finding any of them means scrolling for ages.

Pinch reads the whole chat for you, lists every recipe it finds, and saves the ones you pick to your Library: links, recipes typed into the chat, and photos of recipe cards.

## Step 1: Export the chat from WhatsApp

- **iPhone:** open the chat, tap its name at the top, then **Export chat**.
- **Android:** open the chat, tap **⋮ › More › Export chat**.

To bring the recipe photos too, choose **With all media** on iPhone or **Include media** on Android. **Without media**, the file is smaller, and links and typed recipes still come through.

**Then, on your phone:** pick **Pinch** from the share options. Or save the file, open Pinch, go to **Snap**, tap **Import from a WhatsApp chat**, and choose it.

**On a computer:** send the file to yourself (by email or Drive), then open [pinch-app.io/app](/app/), go to **Snap**, choose **Import from a WhatsApp chat**, and choose the file.

## Step 2: Pick the recipes

Pinch shows what it found, for example "Found 23 possible recipes". Each one says who sent it and when. Filter by **Link**, **Written recipe** or **Photo**, and tick the ones you want, or tap **Select all**. Recipes that are already in your Library are marked, so you don't save them twice.

## Step 3: Import them

Tap **Import**. Pinch saves them one by one and shows how far it is. Keep Pinch open until it's done. You can **Stop** at any time, and the rest stay on the list.

When it's finished, every recipe is in your Library, with its ingredients and steps written out, ready to cook from, scale, and add to your shopping list. If any couldn't be imported, tap **Try the failed ones again**.

## Your chat stays private

Pinch reads the chat **on your device**. The messages, names and photos that aren't recipes never leave your phone or computer. Only the recipes you pick are sent to Pinch to be written out.

## What it costs

Each recipe you import uses one recipe credit, and every account gets **10 free a month**. Pinch shows how many credits the recipes you picked will use. If you pick more than you have, Pinch imports them in order and stops when your credits run out. The rest stay on the list for later, and you can buy more credits that never expire, with no subscription.

Very large chats may hit the daily import limit. If that happens, the rest stay on the list, so you can carry on the next day.

## Common questions

### Do I need an account?

Yes. Sign in first, and the recipes go straight to your Library.

### The file is too big. What now?

Export the chat again and choose **Without media**. Links and typed recipes still come through. For a recipe that's only in a photo, save that photo and add it in **Snap**, like a photo of a cookbook page: see [how to save a recipe from a cookbook photo](/guides/cookbook-photo-to-recipe/).

### Does it work with group chats?

Yes. Groups and one-to-one chats work the same way.

### What happens to links from TikTok or Instagram in the chat?

They're imported like any link you paste into Pinch. See [how to save a recipe from TikTok](/guides/save-tiktok-recipes/).
