Grandma's handwritten cards, a splattered cookbook, a magazine page, a screenshot from a friend: the recipes you love most often aren't online. Typing them out takes forever.

With Pinch, you take a photo, and it writes the recipe out for you: ingredients with amounts and numbered steps, ready to cook from, scale, and add to your shopping list.

## How to do it

1. **Open Pinch and go to Snap.**
2. **Tap "Take a photo"**, or **"Choose a photo"** for one you already have. On a computer, choose **Snap from a photo or PDF**, or drop the photo on the page.
3. **That's it.** The recipe goes straight into your Library. Give it a quick read, and fix anything Pinch misread with **Edit**.

It works with:

- **Cookbook and magazine pages**
- **Handwritten recipe cards**, including old family ones
- **Screenshots** of recipes from messages, social media or websites

## Recipes saved as a PDF

Got a recipe as a PDF, like a scanned magazine page, a printout or a page from a digital cookbook? Choose the PDF the same way you choose a photo. Pinch reads up to 5 pages, under 10 MB. If it can't find a recipe in the PDF, your credit isn't used.

## Tips for a clean result

- **Get the whole recipe in one photo.** Pinch reads one photo per recipe, so fit the ingredients and the steps in the frame. For a recipe split across two pages, start with the page that has the ingredients.
- **Use good light and shoot straight on.** Avoid glare on glossy pages.
- **Make it flat.** Press the book open so the text doesn't curve into the spine.

## Recipes in other languages

Pinch reads recipes in many languages, and you can translate any saved recipe with one tap into English, Spanish, Hebrew, Arabic, Russian, German or French. It's perfect for a family recipe in another language.

## Once it's saved

Your photo recipe works like any other recipe in Pinch:

- **Scale the servings**, or switch between cups and grams.
- **Add the ingredients to your shopping list.**
- **Remix it**, for example vegan, gluten-free or healthier. See [how to make any recipe vegan, gluten-free or dairy-free](/guides/make-any-recipe-vegan-gluten-free/).
- **Cook step by step** in cook mode, with timers.

## What it costs

Each recipe you save uses one recipe credit, and every account gets **10 free a month**. If Pinch can't read a recipe from the photo, you don't pay.

## Common questions

### Can Pinch read handwriting?

Yes, as long as it's legible to you. Neat handwriting in good light gives the best results. Check the recipe once it's saved and fix anything it misread.

### Can I scan a whole cookbook?

You can save as many recipes as you like, one photo per recipe.

### Can I use it on my computer?

Yes. In the browser at [pinch-app.io/app](/app/), choose a photo from your computer, or drop it on the page. No photo on the computer? Choose **Use your phone's camera** and point your phone at the code: the photo you take shows up on your computer, with no app or sign-in needed on the phone.

### What about family recipes in a WhatsApp group?

Pinch can read a whole exported chat and bring in the recipes, the photos of recipe cards too. See [how to save the recipes from a WhatsApp chat](/guides/save-recipes-from-whatsapp/).
