You found a great recipe, but it's full of butter and you're vegan. Or it calls for flour and you cook gluten-free. Swapping one ingredient isn't enough: the steps, the amounts and the cooking times often need to change too.

Pinch's **Remix** adapts a whole recipe to your diet. It rewrites the ingredients and the steps together, while keeping the spirit of the dish.

## Diets you can pick

Open any recipe and tap **Remix**, then pick a style:

- **Healthier** (less fat, sugar and salt)
- **Vegan**
- **Vegetarian**
- **Gluten-free**
- **Dairy-free**
- **Lower carb**
- **High protein**
- **Keto**
- **Mediterranean**
- **Paleo**
- **Pescatarian**
- **Halal**
- **Kosher**

Or type your own request in **What should change?**, for example "for 2, no cream", "nut-free" or "air fryer", and tap **Remix with this**.

## How it works

1. **Save the recipe to Pinch** from a video, a website or a photo.
2. **Tap Remix** under the ingredients and choose a style, or type what you want.
3. **Read "What changed".** Pinch shows a short summary of the swaps and adjustments.
4. **Tap "Use this version"** to keep it, or **"Pick a different style"** to try another.

The recipe updates in place and shows a "{diet} version" label. You can always tap **Revert to original** to get the original recipe back.

## Swap just one ingredient

Don't need a whole new version? Every ingredient has a **Swap** button. Tap it, pick one of the suggested alternatives, and Pinch rewrites the steps that use it. Out of buttermilk, or no eggs in the house? This is the quick fix.

## Make it automatic

If you always cook a certain way, set it once:

1. Go to **Settings → Recipe preferences**.
2. Under **Usually cook**, pick up to 4 diets.
3. Under **Always swap**, add swaps you always make, like "butter → olive oil".
4. Turn on **Adapt recipes I snap automatically**.

Every new recipe you save is then adapted to your diets and swaps as soon as it's saved. You can still revert any recipe to the original.

Your diets also shape the **Cooking Hub**, where recipes that already fit your diets, as written, show up first, and your **meal plan**, which only picks recipes that fit. See [how to plan a week of meals](/guides/weekly-meal-plan/).

## What it costs

Remixes and swaps don't use recipe credits. You can remix each recipe up to 5 times.

## A note on allergies

Remix is great for diets and preferences, but it can't promise a recipe is safe for an allergy or for celiac disease. Always check ingredient labels, especially on packaged foods like broths, sauces and spice mixes, and watch for cross-contamination in your kitchen.

## Common questions

### Can I make a recipe from TikTok or Instagram vegan?

Yes. Save it to Pinch first ([here's how for TikTok](/guides/save-tiktok-recipes/)), then tap Remix and choose Vegan.

### Will a gluten-free version still bake properly?

Remix adjusts the whole recipe, not just the flour, because gluten-free baking often needs different amounts and binders. As with any adapted recipe, the first bake is a test.

### Can I combine diets, like vegan and gluten-free?

Type it in **What should change?**, for example "vegan and gluten-free", and Pinch adapts for both.
