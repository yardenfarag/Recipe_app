You buy milk on the way home. So does your partner. Meanwhile nobody bought the eggs, because they were on a list on someone else's phone.

In Pinch, the people you live with can share **one shopping list and one pantry**. Anyone can add to it, check things off at the store, or see what's already at home, and the changes show up for everyone as they happen.

## Set up a household

1. **Go to Settings → Household**, or tap **Set up a household** on your shopping list.
2. **Give it a name** and pick a picture.
3. **Add your name**, the one the others will see.
4. **Tap "Create household".**

## Invite the people you live with

Send them the invite link: **Send on WhatsApp**, **Share link** or **Copy link**. Standing next to them? Let them point their phone's camera at the QR code.

They open the link, sign in or sign up for a free Pinch account, choose the name the others will see, and tap **Join**. Their list and pantry join yours.

The link works for 7 days. You can **Make a new link** (the old one stops working) or **Stop this link** at any time. A household can have up to 8 people.

## What you share, and what stays yours

**Shared with everyone in the household:**

- **The shopping list.** Each item shows who added it.
- **The pantry**, what's in the fridge, freezer and cupboards. See [what to cook with what's in your fridge](/guides/what-to-cook-with-whats-in-your-fridge/).
- **Always have**, the staples that stay off the list.

**Yours alone:** your recipes, your credits, your meal plan, and your diet and kitchen settings.

So when you tap **Send to list** on your week's plan, the ingredients go on the shared list, and what anyone in the household has at home is left off. See [how to plan a week of meals](/guides/weekly-meal-plan/).

## Shopping together

- **Changes show up live.** Check off the tomatoes, and they're checked on everyone's phone.
- **"I'm going shopping"** tells the others you're heading out, so they can add last-minute things before you get there.
- **Back home,** when you check something off, Pinch offers to add it to the shared pantry.

## Leave, or remove someone

Everyone in a household is equal: anyone can rename it, change its picture, invite, remove someone, or leave.

- **Leave household:** you take a copy of the list and pantry with you. The others keep theirs.
- **Remove someone:** they take a copy of the list and pantry with them, and everyone in the household is told.

## Common questions

### Is it free?

Yes. Households, the shared list and the shared pantry don't use recipe credits.

### Can I be in two households?

No, one at a time. Joining a new household means leaving the one you're in. Its list and pantry stay with the people there.

### Does it work on a computer?

Yes. Open [pinch-app.io/app](/app/) and go to **Settings → Household**. The list updates live in the browser too.

### Can we share recipes too?

Recipes stay in each person's own Library. To send one, open it and tap **Share**: the other person gets a Pinch link and can save their own copy.
