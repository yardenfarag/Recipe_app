Planning a few meals means writing the same things twice ("2 onions… another onion"), and buying olive oil you already have three bottles of.

Pinch builds your shopping list from your recipes. Matching ingredients stack into one line, the list is grouped by supermarket aisle, and what you already have at home stays off it.

## Build a list from several recipes

1. **Open the List tab and tap "From recipes".**
2. **Pick the recipes** you're cooking this week.
3. **Add them.** Matching ingredients stack into one line: two onions from one recipe and one from another become "3 onions."

Pinch tells you what happened, for example "Added from 3 recipes: 12 new, 4 stacked."

To add just one recipe, tap **Add to list** on the recipe and choose the items you need.

## Or send the whole week

If you plan your meals in Pinch, open **Plan** and tap **Send to list**. Every recipe in the week goes on the list in one go, and what you have at home is left off. Tick anything you need more of. See [how to plan a week of meals](/guides/weekly-meal-plan/).

## Shop by aisle

Your list is sorted the way a supermarket is laid out: produce, dairy and eggs, meat, seafood, bakery, pantry, frozen, and spices. Check items off as you go, and they move to a **Checked** section at the bottom.

Amounts come in the units you cook with. Choose metric or US in **Settings**, and the list follows.

## Skip what you already have

Pinch keeps two lists of what's at home, both under **Settings → Pantry**:

- **Always have:** staples you never run out of, like salt, olive oil or rice.
- **Your pantry:** what's in your fridge, freezer and cupboards right now. Take a few photos and Pinch fills it in. See [what to cook with what's in your fridge](/guides/what-to-cook-with-whats-in-your-fridge/).

**Two ways to add a staple:**

- Go to **Settings → Pantry → Always have** and add it.
- Tap **I keep this** on an item when you add a recipe to your list.

**What happens next time:** when you add recipes to your list, your staples are left off or start unticked and marked **In pantry**, so nothing you already have ends up on the list by accident. You can still tick one when you're running low.

A staple covers similar items too, so "salt" also covers "sea salt."

When you check off something you bought, Pinch can add it to your pantry for you, so the pantry stays up to date without another scan.

## Tidy up duplicates

If the same item ends up on your list twice, Pinch marks it **Also listed** and lets you **Combine** the lines or **Keep separate**.

## Shop together

Live with other people? Set up a household and you all share one list and one pantry. Anyone can add to it, everyone sees changes as they happen, and **I'm going shopping** tells the others it's time to add last-minute things. See [how to share a shopping list with your family](/guides/share-shopping-list-with-family/).

## Common questions

### Does it add up amounts?

Yes, when the ingredient and the unit match, like "200 g flour" plus "100 g flour." Different units stay on separate lines so nothing gets mixed up.

### Can I plan from TikTok and Instagram recipes?

Yes. Save the videos to Pinch first ([how to save a recipe from TikTok](/guides/save-tiktok-recipes/)), and they work like any other recipe. For a full method, see [how to make a meal-prep shopping list](/guides/meal-prep-shopping-list/).

### Is it free?

Yes. The shopping list, the pantry, and filling the pantry from photos are free to use.
