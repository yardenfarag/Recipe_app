Every Sunday, the same question: what are we eating this week? You have plenty of saved recipes. Turning them into seven days of meals, without repeating Monday's pasta on Thursday or buying a second bag of spinach, is the part that takes an hour.

Pinch does it in one tap. It fills the week from your recipes, follows your diet, works around what's already in your fridge, and sends the shopping to one list. Planning is free.

## Plan your week

1. **Open Plan** and tap **Plan my week**.
2. **Choose the days and the meals.** Breakfast, lunch and dinner, on the days you pick. Add **snacks** if you want them: no snacks, 1 a day or 2 a day.
3. **Check your diet.** Under **Following**, Pinch shows the diets and allergies from your settings. Tap **Change** to edit them.
4. **Tap "Fill the week".**

Pinch remembers your choices for next time.

## How Pinch picks the recipes

- **Your allergies are never on the plan.** Strict diets, like vegetarian, vegan, halal, kosher, gluten-free and dairy-free, are followed too. Looser ones, like low-carb or keto, are preferred.
- **What's at home comes first.** Recipes that use what's in your pantry rank higher. See [what to cook with what's in your fridge](/guides/what-to-cook-with-whats-in-your-fridge/).
- **Less to buy.** Recipes that share ingredients with another meal that week get a boost, so the cilantro you buy for Monday gets used on Wednesday.
- **Quick on weekdays, longer at the weekend.**
- **No repeats.** No recipe twice in a week, and nothing you cooked in the last two weeks if it can be helped.

Each meal says why it was picked, for example **Uses your spinach**, **Quick: 20 min** or **Not cooked in a while**.

## Only a few recipes saved?

Turn on **Add recipes from the Hub**, and Pinch fills the gaps with recipes from the Cooking Hub that fit your diet. Hub recipes in your plan are saved to your Library, free. Once at least 14 of your own recipes fit, you can plan from your Library alone.

## Make it fit your week

Under **Adjust**, pick what matters this week:

- **Quick weeknights**
- **Cheaper**
- **More protein**
- **Use up my fridge**
- **Fewer new recipes**
- **Skip a food**, for example mushrooms, just for this week

Turn on **Cook extra for lunch**, and a dinner covers the next day's lunch. Pinch adds the extra servings to the recipe for you.

## Change a meal

Don't like a pick? Every meal has quick actions:

- **Another recipe** swaps in a different one.
- **Lock** keeps a meal when you reshuffle.
- **Mark as cooked**, or **Remove from plan**.

**Reshuffle** gives you a fresh week. Locked and cooked meals stay as they are.

You can also add any recipe yourself: open it and tap **Add to plan**, then pick a day and a meal. Tonight's planned dinner shows on your Library, so it's one tap away when it's time to cook.

## Send it to your shopping list

When the week looks right, tap **Send to list**. Every ingredient goes on your shopping list, matching ones stacked into one line and grouped by aisle. What you have at home is left off. Tick anything you need more of. See [a shopping list that knows your pantry](/guides/shopping-list-pantry/).

## Reminders on your phone

In the iPhone and Android apps, Pinch can remind you to take something out of the freezer in the morning, and tells you what's for dinner in the late afternoon. Tap the reminder to open the recipe.

## Common questions

### Is it free?

Yes. Planning the week, reshuffling, and adding Hub recipes to your plan don't use recipe credits.

### How far ahead can I plan?

This week and next week.

### Can I plan on my computer?

Yes. At [pinch-app.io/app](/app/), open **Plan**. On a wide screen you can see the whole week at once, or go day by day. Your plan is on your account, so it's the same on your phone.

### Can my family see the plan?

The plan is yours, but the shopping list it sends to can be shared. Set up a household and everyone shops from the same list. See [how to share a shopping list with your family](/guides/share-shopping-list-with-family/).

### Is it good for meal prep?

Yes. **Cook extra for lunch** turns dinners into next-day lunches. For a full method, see [how to make a meal-prep shopping list](/guides/meal-prep-shopping-list/).
