# Model bake-off, 23 Nov 2026

We re-ran the eval with models that came out on OpenRouter after the 12 Oct run (PIN-44), to see whether a switch would make Pinch better, faster or cheaper. The full tables are in `summary.md`.

Today's production models were re-run on the same day, so every row is comparable. Each case ran once for scores; text and the food-photo check ran 3 times and translate and photo 2 times for latency. Translate and invent were ranked by two judges (Gemini 3.1 Pro and Claude Opus 5.5). Spend: about $23.

## What production used (last 30 days)

Video import was 71% of AI spend ($3.72 of about $5.20), at $0.046 a call. Translate was next ($0.80). The text-model family made about 2,000 calls for $0.40 in total.

## Findings

| Task | Today | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Translate | GPT-6.1 Sol | **Switch to GPT-6.2 Sol** | Best average rank (2.14 vs 2.72), first 43% of the time vs 20%, both judges agree. About 30% faster (9 s vs 13 s), same price. |
| Text extract and classifications | GPT-6 Luna, fallback Qwen 3.8 Flash | **Keep Luna; fallback to Claude Haiku 5.5** | Luna is still the most accurate (100% ingredient recall, perfect appliances and nutrition). Qwen takes 16 s typically and 34 s in slow cases, so every fallback is slow. Haiku: 5 s, 95% recall, no failed calls. |
| Content gate | Mistral Small 4 | **Switch to Inkling 2 Mini, Mistral as fallback** | 30/30 vs 29/30, same 1.7 s, a third of the price. Small gain, low risk. |
| Video extract | Gemini 3.5 Flash | **Muse Ember for uploaded clips; Gemini for YouTube and as the fallback** | Muse Ember had the same ingredient recall (69%; 78% on Hebrew) at $0.0014 a video instead of $0.025, with slightly fewer exact amounts (12% vs 16%). |
| Photo extract | Claude Sonnet 5.5 | Keep | Gemini 4 Hydrogen matched it and was 25% faster, but photo imports are about one a month. |
| Invent | Sonnet 5.5 (photo), GPT-6.1 Sol (text) | Keep | Both are already the top-ranked model for their input. |

All four switches shipped in the follow-up PR. Invent from video stays on Gemini: it wasn't tested, and inventing needs the stronger model.

## Things the numbers depend on

- **YouTube video can only go to Gemini.** Every test video was an Instagram or TikTok clip sent inline. YouTube goes as a URL, which only Gemini (AI Studio) can open, so the video task names Gemini as its `urlVideoModel` and those calls have no fallbacks. In the 30 days before the bake-off, YouTube was 47 of 80 video extracts and $2.71 of $3.69 of video spend, so the saving from Muse Ember is about $1 a month at today's volume, growing with Instagram and TikTok imports.
- **Video amounts are low for every model (8–16%).** Most Reels never show or say the quantities, so the clip alone can't give them. A different model won't fix video imports; more text (transcripts, comments) would.
- **Muse Ember can't be a fallback for the text family.** It rejects reasoning `none`, and a fallback runs at the task's reasoning setting. As a primary it needs `low`. It also returned broken JSON in 5 of 64 appliance answers and 3 of 30 content-gate answers, so it isn't suggested for those.
- **Dropped for reliability:** Tencent Hy4 Flash (25% invalid JSON on text, no macros on nutrition) and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (35% invalid JSON on text). Nemotron 3.5 Super VL and MiniMax M3.1 Flash were clearly worse on video.
- **Latency is wall clock from a laptop.** Earlier runs used llmClient's `durationMs`, which stops at the response headers; that's why the 12 Oct table showed 0.6 s for a whole recipe. Production stores each request's total time (`ai_usage_events.duration_ms`) but not each model call's, so a switch shows up only in the request totals.
