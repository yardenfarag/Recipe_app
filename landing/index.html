How to save a recipe from a video

  1. Find a recipe video. On TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube.
  2. Share it to Pinch. Or copy the link and paste it in.
  3. Get the recipe. Pinch's AI writes out the ingredients and steps, adds a shopping list, and saves it to your library.

Frequently asked questions

What does Pinch do?

Pinch reads a cooking video from TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube and turns it into a written recipe: ingredients, steps, and a shopping list you can check off at the store.

Can Pinch turn a YouTube video into a recipe?

Yes. Paste a YouTube link and Pinch's AI recipe extractor writes out the ingredients and steps, so you don't have to pause and rewind while you cook.

How do I save a recipe from TikTok or Instagram?

Tap Share on the video and pick Pinch, or copy the link and paste it into Pinch. Preview the recipe, then tap save to keep it in your library.

Is Pinch free?

Yes. A free account includes 15 recipe extractions every month. If you need more, you can buy recipe credits in the app. Purchased credits never expire.

Can I use Pinch without the app?

Yes. Pinch also runs in your browser at pinch-app.io/app. Your library syncs when you sign in with the same account.

Which languages does Pinch support?

Pinch is available in English, Spanish, Hebrew, Arabic, and Russian.
