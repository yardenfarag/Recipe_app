Refund Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

If you're not happy with a credit pack, ask for a refund within 14 days of buying it and you'll get your money back in full.

What you can buy

Pinch sells one-time packs of recipe credits. Credits are used for AI actions such as importing a recipe from a video or creating a new one. There are no subscriptions, so there is nothing to cancel and nothing renews.

Purchases on pinch-app.io

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our web orders, and handles payment, invoices and refunds.

  • You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of your purchase, for any reason.
  • To ask, send us a support message with the title Refund, using the email address you paid with. You can also reply to your Paddle receipt or visit paddle.net.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method. Your bank may take 5–10 business days to show it.
  • When a pack is refunded, its credits are removed from your balance.

Purchases in the iPhone or Android app

Apple and Google process in-app purchases, and their refund rules apply. We can't issue these refunds ourselves.

  • App Store: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Google Play: follow Google Play's refund steps.

Free credits

Free monthly credits and other credits we give you at no cost have no cash value and can't be refunded.

Your legal rights

This policy doesn't affect any rights you have under the consumer law where you live.
