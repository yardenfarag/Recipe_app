Full description (both stores, ≤4000):
   The recipe saver without a subscription. Save recipes from TikTok, Instagram & YouTube, and actually cook them.

   10 free recipes every month. Need more? Buy credits only when you want them. No trial, no auto-renew, and your recipes are always yours.

   Pinch is a recipe keeper app for the recipes in your feed. Share a cooking video to Pinch and its AI recipe extractor writes out the ingredients and steps, then puts them on a shopping list. No more pausing, rewinding, and screenshotting.

   READS THE WHOLE VIDEO, NOT JUST THE CAPTION
   Most creators say the amounts out loud or flash them on screen. Pinch watches the video, listens to it, and reads the on-screen text, so you get the whole recipe, every time.

   SAVE RECIPES FROM ANYWHERE
   • Save TikTok recipes and Instagram Reels: tap Share and pick Pinch, or paste the link
   • YouTube video to recipe: ingredients and steps, written out
   • Recipe websites: paste a link and skip the life story
   • Cookbooks and handwritten cards: snap a photo of the page

   A RECIPE TO SHOPPING LIST IN ONE TAP
   • Ingredients from several recipes merge into one line each
   • Grouped by aisle, so you shop the store once
   • Staples you always have stay off the list

   COOK ALONG
   • Keep the source video right beside the steps
   • Step-by-step cook mode with built-in timers
   • Scale servings up or down and the amounts follow

   MAKE IT YOURS
   • Remix any recipe with AI: healthier, vegetarian, gluten-free, and more
   • Swap an ingredient and the steps update to match
   • What's in the fridge? Snap it, and Pinch ranks your saved recipes by what you have

   A RECIPE ORGANIZER THAT SPEAKS YOUR LANGUAGE
   Use Pinch in English, Spanish, Hebrew, Arabic, or Russian, with full right-to-left support for Hebrew and Arabic. Saved a recipe from a video in another language? Translate it into any of those, or into German or French, in one tap.

   YOUR RECIPE LIBRARY, EVERYWHERE
   Keep every saved recipe in one place and group them for dinner, baking, or easy weeknights. Sign in and your library syncs between your phone and the web at pinch-app.io.

   NO SUBSCRIPTION. PAY ONLY FOR WHAT YOU SAVE
   Every account gets 10 free recipe credits each month. Need more? Buy a credit pack in the app. Credits never expire, and there's no subscription, so there's no trial to cancel and nothing renews.

   YOUR RECIPES ARE ALWAYS YOURS
   Credits are only used when Pinch reads a new recipe. Everything you've saved stays in your library to cook and shop from, whether or not you ever buy credits. Export your recipes as a printable PDF cookbook any time.

   PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
   Pinch doesn't sell your personal information. You can delete your account and data at any time in Settings.

   See it. Snap it. Cook it.
