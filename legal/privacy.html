Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Pinch (“we”, “us”) helps you extract structured recipes from social video URLs. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Data we collect

  • Account: email address when you sign up with email, Apple, or Google; optional profile photo.
  • Recipes & library: saved recipes, collections, shopping list items, favorites, and related metadata you create in the app.
  • Credits & AI usage: your recipe credit balance, credit pack purchases, and a ledger of AI actions (import, remix, translation, etc.) used to run free allowances and paid credits.
  • App activity: which screens you open and which buttons you tap, sign-ins and sign-ups, with a random session id, your account or install id, device platform and app version. We never record what you type. Only Pinch admins can see it.
  • Product events: a few milestones, such as saving a recipe or finishing onboarding, with your account or install id and your country (from your network or device region).
  • Guest device limits: a local install identifier and guest extraction/save limits on your device before you create an account.
  • Content you submit: links you paste or share into Pinch, photos you take or pick (such as a cookbook page or your fridge), and text you type, such as a custom remix request or a recipe you write, so we can make and store your recipes.
  • Support & feedback: messages you send us, your email, and device details (app version, OS, device model and language) so we can reply and fix problems.

How we use data

  • Provide recipe extraction, remix, translation, and substitutions.
  • Sync your library across devices when signed in.
  • Enforce free allowances, process credit pack purchases, and prevent abuse.
  • Understand how Pinch is used and diagnose problems, using app activity and product events.
  • Respond to support requests and feedback you send us.

Third parties

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and file storage.
  • OpenRouter — routes AI requests (reading recipes from posts, videos and photos, translation, remixes, substitutions) to the model providers OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Mistral, Alibaba (Qwen) and TypeSafe. We ask OpenRouter to use only providers that do not store or train on request data.
  • ScrapeCreators — metadata for some Instagram/TikTok URLs.
  • Apple / Google — optional sign-in providers.
  • YouTube Data API — optional metadata for YouTube links.
  • RevenueCat — processes credit pack purchases made through Apple and Google; it receives your account id and purchase details.

We do not sell your personal information. Provider processing is limited to operating Pinch.

Retention

We keep account, library and app activity data while your account exists, and delete it when you delete your account. App activity from people who never create an account is linked only to a random install id and has no fixed deletion date. Product events are deleted after 13 months. Aggregated or anonymized usage events may be retained after deletion for cost and abuse analysis. Guest data stays on your device until you clear app data or migrate to an account.

Your choices

  • Delete your account anytime in the app: Settings → Delete account.
  • Or follow the web instructions on Delete your data (keep the account) or Delete your account.
  • Contact us at yarden.farag@gmail.com.

Children

Pinch is not directed at children under 13. Do not create an account if you are under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.
